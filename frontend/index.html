01 · DURABILITY Nothing becomes searchable before it is recoverable. The WAL is fsynced before a write is acknowledged, so losing the process costs milliseconds and no memories.
— MEMORIES RESIDENT
WAL sequence— process generation— supervisor—
Count the memories, kill the process, and count again. SIGKILL runs no handler, flushes no buffer and tidies nothing — which is the only kind of crash worth testing.

03 · ENERGY
— ANSWERS PER 1% OF BATTERY
Latency is the figure everything reports and the wrong one to optimise a battery-powered device against. Answering in 4 ms while holding four cores flat is worse, on a robot, than 12 ms on one.
